Every module, what it owns, what it picks from, and what it produces. The shared reference for founder, Claude and the dev while the admin shell is rebuilt module by module. When something here changes, the page changes.

Governing rule

The whole app should be seamless

The founder's rule, made testable. Every module document is checked against these five.

No dead ends.

Every nav row opens something. Every artefact opens from every horizontal it is tagged with. Every empty state names the next action and links to it.

Pickers are pre-filtered, never full lists.

Candidates come from the junction: allotment, department, roster, free-at-period. "Show all" sits behind a step.

One record, many views.

The same rows serve admin and teacher. Scope changes, the data does not. Never a second table for a second role.

Setup flows into use.

Finishing one setup step offers the next. Data entered once is picked everywhere. Nothing is typed twice.

Tags and provenance are mandatory.

Every artefact row carries its horizontal keys and a link to what it was made from.

Architecture

Horizontals, junctions, verticals

A horizontal is reference data the school agrees on and never carries workflow state. A junction is where two horizontals meet and become one pickable thing. A vertical is a workflow that produces records by picking through junctions. Purple marks what the schema does not have yet.

Subject × Grade sits above Subject × Section: every 6A, 6B and 6C Maths row points at the one Grade 6 Maths row. That single fact is what makes knowledge and syllabus constant across sections, and it is why the Departments & subjects module must land the grade junction, not only the department link.

Behaviour

Pick rules

The founder's example, generalised. Each rule is a filter over a junction, and each is an acceptance test in the module that implements it.

Timetable cell, 6A Maths, Wednesday P3: who teaches it?
Tier 1Teachers with a current allotment on 6A Maths. Usually one. Pre-selected.
Tier 2Teachers in the department that owns Maths. Offered as "also in Mathematics", not selected.
Tier 3Anyone, behind "show all". The slot is flagged as outside allotment.
AlwaysBlocked: a teacher already placed elsewhere at that period; a room already taken.
Allotment board, 6A Maths: who can be allotted?

Default list is the Maths department. Anyone else behind "show all". Load shown per teacher from current allotments and timetable.

Knowledge for 6A Maths

Is the Grade 6 Maths set. A teacher adds a section-only extra by attaching to 6A Maths; it never leaks to 6B.

A fill request for 6A Maths, Wednesday P3

Candidates are teachers with no slot at that period, same department first, then anyone free. Without a published timetable the list degrades to the plain department list.

A question paper for 6A Maths

Draws from rule 3's set, so 6A and 6B papers share sources unless the section added extras. The paper stores which sources it used.

Every vertical row dates from the year

Directly or through its class. Rollover never rewrites verticals: it creates next year's classes and memberships and marks the old year read-only.

Decisions

09 SeptNever commit or push to main in the product repos. One branch per logical change, pull request when verified.
10 SeptBuild as admin-only for now; teacher-facing screens untouched. Shell first, modules after.
10 SeptSetup is about the school (another admin sees the same value). Settings is about the app and the account. Settings stays in the user menu.
10 SeptUnbuilt modules stay in the nav as Soon rows landing on a placeholder with the module's own description.
10 SeptTimetable is two layers: period templates per grade (Setup, configuration) and the filled grid per section (Classes, data). Templates are rows, not code.
10 SeptKnowledge is two things: class knowledge at grade × subject inherited by sections, and the teacher-shared library. Separate pages, never confused.
10 SeptHome stays role-driven with an admin View-as switch. Teacher home kept, not scrapped.
10 SeptSubjects & books becomes Departments & subjects. Head of department is a marker with department-scoped permissions, not a third role. Books are materials on a subject per grade, managed in Class knowledge.
10 SeptThe whole app should be seamless. Five testable rules above govern every module document.
10 SeptOpening a new academic year is the first step of rollover, not a separate action.
